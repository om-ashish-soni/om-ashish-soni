# 2030 — Apex

**Theme.** Execute on the moat. Four years of compounded depth converge into contributions, not coursework.

**AI integration.** At this point the distinction collapses. You are an AI-infrastructure engineer who can reason from transistor to transformer to production cluster.

**End-of-year ceiling.**
- Hired at a tier-1 AI infra org (vLLM team, NVIDIA, Anthropic infra, OpenAI infra, Together, Groq, Tenstorrent, Modal, Anyscale, Cerebras, Databricks, DeepMind systems), **or** founding a credible AI-infra startup, **or** staff-level at a research lab.

- Recognized OSS contributor with merged commits across ≥2 major projects.

## What You Work On

By 2030 you should be able to **pick your problem**. Candidate directions, any one of which is a career:

1. **Novel inference kernel** — a fused attention variant, a new KV compression scheme, a new sparse decoding trick.
2. **AI compiler contribution** — a passes or optimization landed in Triton, MLIR, torch.compile, XLA.
3. **Distributed training optimization** — a new communication primitive, a scheduler for heterogeneous clusters.
4. **Hardware-software co-design** — a custom operator that benefits from a specific silicon feature, measured on real hardware.
5. **Systems-for-agents** — infrastructure that makes agent systems (like mo-code) 10× faster, cheaper, or more reliable.

Pick one. Commit. Ship hard.

## Research & Publication Track

### Papers to submit (aim for one)
- **MLSys** — the intersection venue; accepts systems-for-ML work.
- **OSDI / SOSP** — top systems; high bar, high signal.
- **ASPLOS / ISCA / HPCA / MICRO** — architecture.
- **NSDI / SIGCOMM** — networking.
- Workshop tracks of the above — realistic stretch for a solo/small-group effort.

### How to do this with Claude Max as a co-author-grade companion
- Weekly lit reviews: Claude summarizes 10 papers, you pick 3 to read in depth.
- Experiment design: Claude attacks your methodology before you run the experiment — cheaper than a failed run.
- Writing: you draft, Claude reviews like a hostile reviewer. Rewrite until Claude cannot find a hole.

### Realistic caveat
Solo first-author acceptance at top venues is hard in one year. If you don't land the top venue, **land the workshop + preprint + working code + blog series**. That combination is often career-equivalent.

## What NOT to Do in 2030

- Don't start new foundations. You had four years for that.
- Don't spread across all five directions above. Pick one, commit.
- Don't undersell. The artifacts speak — price yourself accordingly.
- Don't hide. Ship in public, loudly.
- Don't skip the writing. A paper + blog series is force-multiplier for every job / startup / collaboration conversation.
- Don't burn out. Four years of 04:00-10:00 is real wear. Protect sleep, exercise, relationships. The apex year requires reserves.
